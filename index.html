About Me

Researcher, builder, and educator working across AI and biomedicine.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Dakota. My work focuses on developing machine learning and deep learning frameworks that integrate imaging, spectroscopy, and biomedical literature to support disease detection, prognosis, and real-time decision support.

My current research includes spatial gene expression upscaling from histology, ontology-guided literature mining, multimodal spectroscopy for food quality assessment, and interpretable biomedical AI systems.

Current Focus

  • Visium v2 to Visium HD spatial gene expression upscaling
  • Ignet literature mining and gene interaction networks
  • Histology, spectroscopy, and multimodal biomedical data
  • Interpretable deep learning for diagnostic research
