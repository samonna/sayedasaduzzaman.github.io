About Me

Researcher, builder, and educator at the intersection of AI and biomedicine.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Dakota. My work focuses on developing machine learning and deep learning frameworks that integrate histology imaging, spectroscopy, and biomedical literature to support disease detection, prognosis, and real-time decision support.

Open to research collaborations. Reach out via email or ResearchGate.

My current research centers on spatial gene expression upscaling from H&E histology, ontology-guided PubMed literature mining, and interpretable biomedical AI. I also hold a faculty position as Assistant Professor (on study leave) at Rangamati Science and Technology University, Bangladesh.

Current Focus

  • Visium v2 to Visium HD spatial gene expression upscaling (SCALE-ST)
  • Ignet — PubMed-scale gene interaction network mining
  • H&E histology and multimodal biomedical data fusion
  • Interpretable deep learning for computational pathology
  • LLM/NLP applications in biomedical literature mining

Research Areas

A portfolio centered on biomedical AI and computational discovery

Spatial Transcriptomics

Histology-guided gene expression prediction, Visium HD upscaling via Gaussian-weighted patch anchoring, sub-spot modeling, and morphology-to-molecular learning.

Biomedical Imaging

Deep learning pipelines for histopathology, MRI classification, hyperspectral imaging, burn severity analysis, and plant disease detection.

Multimode Spectroscopy

Machine learning for spectral biomarkers, food freshness assessment, adulteration detection, quality control, and non-destructive sensing systems.

Biomedical NLP & Knowledge Graphs

PubMed-scale literature mining, fine-tuned LLMs (Llama, Gemma, Mistral, Qwen), ontology-guided discovery, and gene interaction network visualization.

Featured Work

Selected research and software projects

Featured · Active Research

SCALE-ST — Spatial Gene Expression Upscaling

Multi-scale deep learning framework using ViT-Large/UNI backbone with Gaussian-weighted patch anchoring to predict high-definition spatial gene expression from H&E histology. Validated against Visium HD data at 2/8/16 µm resolutions using a ZINB loss formulation.

Spatial TranscriptomicsViT-LargeVisium HDZINB
Platform

Ignet — Literature Mining & Gene Networks

Web-based gene interaction network platform mining the full PubMed corpus using SciMiner NLP. Provides centrality metrics, domain ontologies, and interactive visualization of gene regulatory networks.

PubMedSciMiner NLPGene NetworksMySQL
Spectroscopy

Multimode Spectroscopy for Food Quality

AI framework combining VIS-NIR, SWIR, and fluorescence spectral data to assess meat freshness, identify biomarkers, and support non-destructive food quality assessment in collaboration with USDA partners.
